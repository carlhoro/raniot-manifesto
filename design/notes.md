This site is conceived as a professional manifesto.
It is not a product, not a services page, and not a marketing artifact.
Its purpose is to present a clear position trough tecnology and digital transformation

The initial version generated with V0 intentionally establishes only a basic visual scheme:
typography, color constraints, and a minimal structural layout.
This baseline is deliberately simple in order to preserve full control over
design decisions, layout evolution, and component composition at later stages.

V0 is used as a starting point, not as a design authority.
Its role is to provide a neutral foundation upon which structure, hierarchy,
and editorial depth can be built intentionally through code.

Key design intentions:

- The page functions as a manifesto, not as a narrative biography.
- Content and structure take precedence over visual expressiveness.
- The design must support long-form reading and thoughtful navigation.
- Visual elements should emerge from editorial needs, not from trends.
- Component design is a means to reinforce meaning, not to decorate content.
- The site should feel stable, deliberate, and intellectually grounded.

Initial observations from the baseline implementation:

- The tone and typography are appropriate, but the visual rhythm is too flat.
- Sections are conceptually distinct but visually similar.
- There is a lack of editorial anchors that guide the reader.
- The hero section introduces context but lacks declarative force.
- References are structurally correct but visually underemphasized.

These notes are intentionally open-ended.
They serve as a working log to guide iteration, refactoring, and refinement
as the site evolves from a structural baseline to a controlled editorial composition.
