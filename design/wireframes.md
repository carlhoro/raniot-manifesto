This document defines the conceptual and structural layout of the site.
It does not prescribe final styles, animations, or visual details.
Its purpose is to describe the editorial flow and the role of each section
within a single vertical reading experience.

The site is structured as a single-page, vertically scrolling document.

Visual styles and UI components will be adapted from Tailwind-based component libraries
(e.g. Flowbite or similar), but components will not be copied verbatim.
Each component will be simplified, adjusted, or recomposed to serve
the specific editorial intent of the section in which it appears.
